## Regal and the other landmarks

The Regal compatibility proxy was removed. The solid exterior now follows the model's own projected ground-accessible geometry. Collision clips walls, columns and sloped details against local terrain heights. The former white-columned local-Z cutoff removed real wall faces together with the arcade. It was removed and the modeled walls and columns now determine collision. Horizontal paving stays walkable. The two Circuit Court misses came from using one base height across sloped ground. All 15 Regal, seven white-columned and two Circuit Court misses cleared.

The model GLBs and their transforms are unchanged. Collision remains a merged one-metre grid for landmarks. Thin architectural details and overhangs may still be approximate, and buildings have no playable interiors.

The elevated span is represented at the same plan coordinates, and shots and the camera query its elevated volume independently of ground navigation. The game uses a provisional 4.8 m clearance above its highest sampled perimeter terrain and a 1.2 m deck thickness. These are gameplay dimensions. A surveyed deck elevation, pier-level detail, and bridge-deck traversal remain outside this repair.

## Repeated 24-zombie profiling

Chromium 131 / SwiftShader, High preset, 480 × 270, same camera and 24 staged zombies with fixed starting positions and speed. Each run uses a fresh browser context, frame-boundary GL completion waits, six warm-up frames and 24 measured frames. These are matched 007B tests against `07fc6c2b` and use a different camera/fixture from the earlier 583/683 ms samples.

| Build and repetition | Frame median / P95, ms | Nav median / P95, ms | Collision queries median / P95 | Median draw calls |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Initial PR #16, A | 699.9 / 1,483.3 | 0.10 / 0.30 | 1,023 / 1,117 | 455 |
| 007B, A | 583.4 / 2,016.6 | 0.10 / 0.20 | 770 / 864 | 455 |
| Initial PR #16, B | 99.9 / 2,366.6 | 0.10 / 0.20 | 1,023 / 1,117 | 455 |
| 007B, B | 466.6 / 1,966.5 | 0.10 / 0.30 | 770 / 864 | 455 |

The largest candidate gap was 2,833 ms. Its logged navigation increment was 0.0 ms at browser timer precision and animation increment was 0.7 ms. The paired P95 comparisons went in opposite directions. The runs do not reproduce a consistent tail increase attributable to collision or navigation. Hardware GPU timing and the cause of the remaining software-rendered frame gaps were not established.

Matching runs preserve draw counts, shadows, population and preset. The small triangle difference comes from road edits and the replacement bridge representation. The collision broad phase now uses 16 m buckets and an axis-aligned fast path for known rectangles, while retaining the prior radius and polygon tests. Seven repeated batches of 25,000 fixed city queries had medians of 2.2–2.5 ms on the initial PR and 1.6–1.9 ms in 007B. The corresponding forced two-window route builds were 13.3–16.4 ms versus 9.6–11.0 ms. The city geometry also changes between revisions, so these microbenchmarks measure the combined result.
