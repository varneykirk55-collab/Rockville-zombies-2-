# Rockville Zombies 2 · Historic Red Brick Courthouse

**Stage 1 architectural specification · Awaiting approval**  
Target building: the 1891 Red Brick Courthouse at **29 Courthouse Square**, designed by Frank E. Davis in the Romanesque Revival style. The tower and brick exterior in IMG_1503 define the asset. IMG_1504 shows the separate 1931 limestone Grey Courthouse at 27 Courthouse Square and helps identify the west junction. Keep the red-brick building and a short documented connector termination within the asset boundary. The Grey Courthouse, modern court buildings, landscaping, street furniture, and surrounding square are outside this asset. ## Roof and tower silhouette

- **Main roof.** Slate hipped roof running along the long axis, with prominent east and west cross gables. Preserve the shallower roof planes near the wing corners, the projecting gable shoulders, the slight flare at the eaves, and the dentil line underneath. The southeast HABS angle also shows the smaller rear gabled bay and a broad masonry chimney above the rear roof. Set chimney placement from the angled views.
- **Pitch estimates.** Use about **28–37°** as a provisional main hip range and **35–43°** for east and west cross gables. Use **48–55°** for the tall slate pyramid on the tower. These are silhouette blockout ranges inferred from photos. Fit the eaves, gable apexes, and tower roof against the references before settling on values.
- **Tower crown.** Keep masonry corner piers, a dark open stage with **two sandstone columnettes per face**, horizontal ledges and small projecting spouts. The **quarter-divided circular window** belongs in the broad brick field below the open stage. Reproduce its visible cross-shaped divisions. The slate pyramid ends in a small copper-clad apex and finial. The upper copper-clad railings are recorded on **three sides**. Match their photographed attachment points. The square-pattern railing immediately over the main doors is a separate lower feature.

## Stage 2 acceptance target

The silhouette checks are the centered square tower, open crown, slate pyramid and copper finial, quartered oculus and stacked window tiers, flared hipped roof with cross gables, and stepped granite entry. Keep hard edges, distinct volumes, restrained saturation, and legible window recesses. Give the masonry the environmental density of a GTA V streetscape and preserve the clean long-distance reads of classic Call of Duty Zombies.
